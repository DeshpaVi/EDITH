Hotel Room Service Menu

Room service is available 24 hours a day. Orders are delivered to the guest's room.
Every order gets an order ID. Preparation and delivery time depends on the dish.
Each extra portion of the same dish adds about 5 minutes.

Pizza - about 25 minutes
Burger - about 15 minutes
Noodles - about 15 minutes
Fried rice - about 15 minutes
Pasta - about 20 minutes
Sandwich - about 10 minutes
Salad - about 10 minutes
Soup - about 10 minutes

Chef special: the Blue Heron platter is served only on Fridays and costs 4321 rupees.
